Data Matters

When I think of my calling as an Engineer, I usually think of someone that loves to build and improve processes till the end of time. However, on the other side of the boxing ring, I must always stress to myself, that in this path, two North Stars can be meaningful at the same time, because, as the title praises, “Data Matters”. 

In my current job, one of the founders has a culture of data priority. And, to be honest, much to my chagrin, I’ve started to feel that he is totally spot on. This is a short list of some of the insights I’ve learned here.

1. You should be able to understand what happened by reading the data, not the code.
2. Bugs should be fixed in your code and then in your database.
3. People outside yourself are using this data to gain knowledge.
4. You should modify the deprecated data structure to comply with your changes.
5. Your data structure should be as elegant as your code.
6. Tread carefully with updates.

And the list can keep on growing, but just to sum up, let me include one picky rule we have. **When you screw up, you are responsible for fixing the database.** This is a heartbreaking, horrible and painstakingly dawdling task. You should fix all the information, probably by creating a script that picks up all the broken pieces and builds the correct numbers. I totally loathe it, but you know, it helps you be alert in your next challenge. 

So remember, just keep yourself focused on the data, and don’t rely only on your code because, inside that database, you can also find your real value. 

Hope it helps!!
